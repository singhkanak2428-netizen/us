16 · TO YOU

Dear Mohit,

I don't know what's the definition of love, but I guess after spending 365 days with you, in which humne pyaar dekha, rona dekha, ek dusre se alag hoke bhi dekha, lekin har baar hum ek dusre ke aur qareeb aa gaye... warna jise ek baar chhod do, usse dobara baat toh kya, dekhne tak ka mann nahi karta. Toh mere liye toh yahi pyaar hai.


Aur ek aur bhi cheez hai. Insaan jiske saath hass le, usse wo bhula sakta hai, lekin jiske saath ro le, wo usse kabhi nahi bhula sakta. Ye bhi pyaar hai.


Pyaar, pyaar, pyaar... Tumhare saath har cheez mein pyaar hai. Kyuki tum hi mera pyaar ho.


Tum mujhe apna pyaar maano ya na maano, but mera pyaar tum hi ho aur rahoge.


Pehle darti thi ye kehne se, lekin ab nahi darti. Mai akele reh lungi, par tumhare alawa kisi aur ke saath kabhi nahi reh paungi, kyuki mai jhoothi nahi hu aur tumhare alawa kisi aur ko dekhne tak ka mann nahi karta mera.


Yaar tum baat karte ho ki mujhe kisi aur se pyaar ho jayega... huh. Tum bhi yahi ho, mai bhi yahi. Dekh lena.


I'll always wait for you. Jao, azaad ho tum mujhse. Jo tumhe karna hai karo. I'll support you in everything.


But ye kabhi galti se bhi mat sochna ki mai tumhe pyaar karna chhod dungi.


Whatever this is, I hope we never stop finding our way back to each other.


Happy first anniversary. ❤️
